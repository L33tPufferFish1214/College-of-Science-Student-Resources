## Reusable Department Pass

For each department, check whether the official site has student-facing pages for:

- Academic advising, appointments, and drop-in help
- Permission codes, course overrides, forms, petitions, and placement
- Tutoring, study rooms, labs, or course help rooms
- Scholarships, awards, emergency funding, and application deadlines
- Research, internships, REUs, lab placement, and faculty mentor matching
- Student organizations, engagement, mentoring, outreach, and community groups
- Jobs, TA roles, lab roles, peer mentor roles, and career-facing opportunities
- Safety, field work, lab access, equipment, room reservations, or required training
- Transfer, AP/IB/prior-credit, new-student, and first-year requirements

Implementation pattern:

- Add direct action cards for high-frequency student tasks.
- Add `department` to each department-specific card.
- Add `sourceUrl` and `sourceLabel` when a card is derived from a department website.
- Keep one broad Department Hub card as the fallback route.
- Add regression search terms for anything survey-backed or easy to lose.
